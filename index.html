Playable Ideas

Small toys for trying out musical ideas by hand.

Each toy explores one idea. Its spec is written for AI agents: enough to rebuild the toy from scratch, in any language or on any hardware.
The toys are built for a large screen. On a phone, they offer a preview.

Motif and memory

Creating musical motifs out of random events.

  • motif-memory Memorise the last two bars, replay them, and hear how far you can degrade them.
  • markov Chain events by probability, with one step of memory.
  • statistical-feedback Dice with memories.
  • branches Grow motifs as branches, then weigh them.

These toys are part of a bigger project: exploring fuzziness and fussiness in instrument design, to start a dialogue with other musicians. Next could be strategies for field recordings, virtualisation, or whatever you are interested in. Tell me below.
