## Rules that now apply to every layer

1. **The nameplate (`shows`) is a phone line.** It is the first thing a thumb
   reads, it is the same string on desktop and phone, and it may not name a
   control the phone does not show (no MEM, cell, build, weights, memory
   switch, write, heavier). It carries the claim or the question; the gesture
   lives in the state line. Nobody takes the 30-word allowance on
   statistical-feedback: ≤ 20 words on every page.
2. **The phone `running` line carries a listening cue** inside 92 characters
   (the phone's standing text sits below the share button, so this is the
   only sentence that reaches the ear in 30 seconds). One gesture, then what
   to listen for.
3. **Rewrite the phone `shut` sentence.** The live "muted, or at zero, on the
   desktop page" survived three writers; it must not survive four. One plain
   sentence; it may be the same on all four pages. It must not say "unmute"
   alone, because the master at zero is the other cause.
4. **Plain declaratives on the phone, your style's voice at the desk.** The
   editor's verdict: the register that works changes with the reader. The
   phone layers (shows, teaser.words, teaser.text, share) are read cold at
   11.5 px with the speaker talking; the desktop text and the start.words may
   carry the argument.
5. **Drop the "(N)" character counts from the copy lines**; the cop measures.
   Put the counts in `## notes` if you want them recorded.

## Errors every draft shares with the live pages (fix them all)

- statistical-feedback `start.nothing`: "or open silence" is **wrong**.
  Silence's weight is renormalised from the voices' (spec §2.3), so with every
  voice at zero nothing can be chosen whatever silence is set to. The remedy
  is a voice's weight.
- markov `start.running.one`: "tap a cell in its rest row" is **wrong** when
  the stalled walker stands on a sound. The remedy is the row its lit strip
  marks.
- "memory off" turns "the ledger / the books off" or "into dice" (meta, og,
  share): **wrong**. The books are always kept; memory off makes selection
  stop reading them. The question draft's og had it right.
- motif-memory: the amount of music is "held", not "never moves" / "stays as
  dense" (spec §3.5 measures it failing for the kick at rate 1).
- markov standing lines must not assume the opening example ("both walkers
  start", "a style arrives", "the example" in teaser.text): the spec's rule
  is that no standing line states what a matrix holds.
- possibles: heavier → "more often" and bias-right → "the light branches
  win" are true only on the left half of the bias range. Say what is true
  across the range, or say "at the default".
- "the machine has it on the next cell": it is in the machine's *menu* from
  the next cell; the sound comes when the walk next stands there.
